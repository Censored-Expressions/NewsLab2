# Writer Reasoning Hard Gate Teaching

Updated: 2026-08-08

## Rule
The Writer may not decide what the story is. The locked Story Dossier and Story Understanding decide the event; Writer Reasoning decides how to explain it; the Writer only executes the approved plan.

## Required Execution Path
Locked Dossier Revision -> Story Understanding -> Writer Reasoning Contract -> Reasoning Validation -> Paragraph Plan -> Draft -> Sentence-to-Fact Verification -> Headline -> Editor.

## New Hard Checks
- Alternative-event check: if promoted facts can describe two different events, split or rebuild the dossier.
- Evidence-removal check: if removing the weakest source breaks the central event, downgrade to a brief or hold.
- Headline truth check: actor, action, and consequence must be supported by promoted facts before the headline can publish.

## Fallback Boundary
Generic body/headline fallback is allowed only for developing briefs with enough verified facts. Standard and deep articles must return to Writer Reasoning or Dossier Recovery when the plan, body, verification, or headline fails.

## Metrics To Watch
- reasoning plans created before prose
- draft fact coverage
- paragraph-plan alignment
- unsupported factual sentences
- headline/lead mismatch
- story identity mismatch
- standard article first-pass approval
- repair loops per article
